Shipping

EGO currently delivers within Egypt only.

A flat shipping fee of EGP 75.00 is added to every order, once per order regardless of how many items or how many of each you order.

All orders are Cash on Delivery — you pay in cash when your order arrives.

Delivery timeframe: Orders are currently expected to be delivered within 5–7 business days within Egypt. Delivery timeframes may be updated as our operations develop.
