Returns & Exchanges

We want you to feel confident about every order. Here's how returns and exchanges work:

  • Return window: 14 days from the date of delivery.
  • Exchange window: 14 days from the date of delivery.
  • Eligibility: Items must be unused, unwashed, in their original condition, with tags attached.
  • Refund method: Bank transfer, issued after the returned item is received and the return is approved.
  • Return shipping: The customer is responsible for return shipping costs when returning an item due to change of mind or size.

Have a question about an order or want to start a return? Contact us and we'll help directly.
